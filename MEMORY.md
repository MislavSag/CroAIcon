## Facts that do not change

- The stack is R for analysis, Quarto for posts, git for everything.
- The GFI base is FINA annual reports, table `db_afs`, one row per firm and year.
- Trusted baseline columns are `employeecounteop` and `nacerev21`. Financial columns require the `codes_gfi_db_afs_physical` codebook plus an analysis-specific audit before use.

## Data quirks

- [LEARN:data] A rising firm count in `db_afs` partly reflects wider coverage of the base, not real growth → say coverage, not growth, unless you can separate the two.
- [LEARN:data] Employee counts are end of period headcounts and more robust than firm counts → lead with employment when the two disagree.
- [LEARN:data] `codes_gfi` does not match the physical `db_afs.bNNN` financial-column layout -> use MySQL table `codes_gfi_db_afs_physical`, imported from `financije_sifrarnik.xlsx`, for GFI balance/P&L column labels.
- [LEARN:data] The VIEW `vw_db_afs_metric_catalog` is ALSO poisoned — `SHOW CREATE VIEW` shows it is built on `codes_gfi` (`from codes_gfi cg`), so it inherits the wrong bNNN map. Never resolve `db_afs` column meaning from it. Verified column map + the empirical proof (physical `b110`=revenue 88% cov €105bn vs catalog `b125` 7% €0.8bn; `b061`/`b063` assets/equity ~100% vs `b065`/`b067` ~6%; negative equity is `b063<0`≈28-32%, NOT `b067` which is floored at 0) live in `_workflow/gfi-variable-map.md` — the single source of truth. Cheap check: a real headline line is populated for most firms and sums to a sane macro total; a wrong column covers ~5-8% and sums to nonsense. Taught by a session that used `b125`/`b065`/`b067` off the catalog view and got a fake "financials only cover 8k firms / no negative equity" result.
- [LEARN:data] Godišnje izvješće o državnim potporama za 2024. mijenja metodologiju. Prvi put odvojeno prikazuje poljoprivredu i ribarstvo i izuzima izravna plaćanja ZPP-a (842,1 mil. eura) iz definicije potpore → naslovni iznos za 2024. (1,4 mlrd.) NIJE usporediv s naslovnim iznosom izvješća za 2023. (3.035,6 mil.). Isto izvješće 2023. iskazuje ponovno na blizu 2,1 mlrd. i pad mjeri na 30,4%. Naivna usporedba naslovnih iznosa daje lažnih minus 54%. Izvješće za 2024. nije na `mfin.gov.hr`, nego samo na sabor.hr i u materijalima 135. sjednice Vlade.
- [LEARN:data] `db_afs` has far more geography and activity detail than `_workflow/gfi-variable-map.md` documents. **`municipalityid`** is 100% covered, 556 units, indexed, with name lookups in `codes_municipal` (`municipalID`, `naziv`) and `ref_municipality`. **`nacerev22` / `nacerev23` / `nacerev24`** (NKD division / group / class) are 100% covered per firm-year back to 1995, and are genuinely per-year, not a backfilled snapshot (3% of section I firms change `nacerev24` over a decade). The snapshot version is `subjekti_current.nkd2007`, a different thing. Two traps. NKD **79 sits in section N**, not I, so a `nacerev21='I'` filter silently drops all travel agencies. And `nacerev22/23` are **not indexed**, so every query must lead with `nacerev21` + `reportyear` or it full-scans 2,3M rows and times out. Taught building the turizam post.
- [LEARN:data] `codes_municipal.naziv` and `codes_nkd2007.OPIS_DJEL` are stored **latin2**, not UTF-8. Connect with `charset="latin2"` or every Croatian name comes back mojibake (`Andrija?evci`, `Smje?taj`). The `db_afs` numeric columns are unaffected either way.
- [LEARN:data] GFI cannot measure seasonality. `employeecounthw / employeecounteop` is ~1,0 for coastal hotels, identical to manufacturing, and `monthofoperation` is 12 for 92% of firms. Neither field carries a summer signal → take seasonality from DZS monthly statistics, never estimate a *true* seasonal workforce from the financial statements.
- [LEARN:data] Firm revenue in `db_afs` is EUR across the whole span, including the kuna years (2013 reads 1.451,8 M for NKD 55, not a 7,5x kuna number), so no currency conversion is needed. But `price_deflator` is **NULL for the last year** (2024 as of this writing), so any real-terms series ends one year short of the nominal one.
- [LEARN:data] Municipality-level *revenue* is distorted by head-office booking, county-level is not. Big coastal groups book a whole neighbourhood at their own address, so Vrsar reads 0,59 EUR per night on 1,75 M nights while Maistra books its Vrsar camps in Rovinj. Inside a county the displacement nets out. Use county for anything about money per place, municipality only for counts. Checking the top of a ranking is not enough to clear this, the distortion shows at the bottom.
- [LEARN:data] DZS `BS_TU14` dijeli noćenja po **vrsti smještajnog objekta**, nikada po tome vodi li objekt firma ili kućanstvo. Udio skupine 55.2 u noćenjima (49,8% za 2024.) zato **nije** udio kućanstava, a podjela firma/kućanstvo se iz statistike turizma ne može izmjeriti, ni iz eksperimentalne statistike platformi, ni iz primorskih i otočnih tablica. Prihod trgovačkih društava u 55.2 podijeljen s noćenjima te skupine (4,4 EUR) nije ni cijena ni stopa naplate, nego artefakt dviju različitih populacija → za 55.2 ne računati eurske iznose po noćenju, a udio noćenja i udio prihoda iznositi kao dvije odvojene raspodjele. Ista tablica ima i drugu razinu hijerarhije (uvlaka u nazivu), koja se zbraja u ukupno bez ostatka i dopušta da se nazivnik nazove točno: *sobe, apartmani i kuće za odmor* su 45,2 od 46,6 mil. noćenja skupine i 48,2% državnih, ostatak su hosteli, lječilišta, prenoćišta i domovi. Naučeno ispravkom posta `posts/2026-07-noc-bez-cijene/`.
- [LEARN:data] GFI/FINA health employment (zdravstvo, NKD područje Q) does not match DZS health figures → DZS counts all health personnel plus dental care and pharmaceutical wholesalers (veledrogerije), a wider scope than the GFI base. Read the health sector and its +164% gain cautiously. Flagged by Zoran Aralica on the sektori post.
- [LEARN:data] The GDP long-run post claimed its socialist stretch rests on "jugoslavenske stope prenesene na Hrvatsku" → wrong. MPD 2023 carries Croatia 1952-2008 as Milanović's republic-level reconstruction (its own Sources sheet says so, and the pre-1990 growth rates differ from every other Yugoslav republic in every single year, checked from `data/raw/maddison/mpd2023.xlsx`). The real caveats for that stretch are the *društveni proizvod* vs BDP concept gap (BDP ~12-14% larger; Dubey 1975, Miljković 1991 via Bićanić, Deskar-Škrbić i Zrnc, HUB 2016) and the 1990 anchor level ("sidro"). Verify provenance claims against the source's own documentation before printing them. Taught reworking `posts/2026-06-hrvatski-rast-dugi-niz`.

- [LEARN:style] Sensational, anthropomorphic verbs landed on abstract financial concepts (*omjer vrišti*, *dug jede prostor*, *dug boli* in a header, *slika puca*) and dramatic words replaced plain ones (*masovan*, *novi projekt*) → keep motion verbs for concrete actors (sektori, firme, radnici) and give abstract concepts (dug, omjeri, marža, slika) calm, literal language even when the number is big; prefer the exact word (*velik*, *uzima prostor za nove investicije*, header *koči ulaganje* not *boli*). See the *Calm words for abstract things* section in `_workflow/house-style-guide.md`. Taught editing `posts/2026-06-zaduzenost-hrvatskih-firmi/index.qmd`.
- [LEARN:data] An era average can erase a major crisis: the long-run GDP post counted six falls while its own spliced series held a seventh, the 1929-1932 depression (minus 17,6%, deeper than the counted Informbiro drop), smoothed away inside the interwar average of plus 1,8% and visible but unnamed on the chart → before a post claims how many falls a series has, run the drawdown census (`scripts/gdp_drawdowns.R` → `outputs/tables/gdp_drawdowns.csv`, every peak/trough/recovery) and state the counting threshold explicitly. ## Workflow learnings

- [LEARN:chart] `fill_between` and per-segment `plot` calls silently interpolate across missing years (the uncertainty ribbon drew straight through both war gaps) or fabricate a one-year hole where adjacent segments meet (soft `<= 1951` next to firm `>= 1952` left 1951-1952 drawn by neither; same at the 1990s reconstruction edges) → draw ribbons and lines on a complete year grid so NaN breaks the fill exactly at real gaps, and let adjacent segments share their boundary year. Fixed in `python/gdp_charts.py` (`ribbon_grid()`, shared seam years). The same bug survived unnoticed in `raw_panels()` (Tica panel drew one unbroken line through both war gaps) until the 2026-07-25 QA pass → when fixing a bug class, grep the whole file for every other plot call, not just the one that was flagged.
- [LEARN:workflow] `draft: true` uz Quartov zadani `draft-mode: gone` briše tijelo posta i renderira prazan HTML od ~90 bajtova, pa nacrt izgleda kao bijela stranica i u `_site` i u `quarto preview` → u `_quarto.yml` pod `website:` stoji `draft-mode: unlinked`. Nacrt se tada renderira u cijelosti, a i dalje ne ulazi u popise, tražilicu ni RSS. Ne vraćati na `gone` jer se nacrt onda ne može pročitati prije objave.
- [LEARN:workflow] `quarto render` i `quarto preview` na postovima s Python chunkovima trebaju `QUARTO_PYTHON=.venv/Scripts/python.exe`. Bez toga Quarto uzme sistemski Python (3.14), koji nema jupyter ni yaml, i render padne na *Jupyter is not available*.
- [LEARN:workflow] Pomoćnik `hr_number` u postovima stavlja tisućice, pa godinu ispisuje kao *2.025.* → za godine koristiti zaseban `year()` pomoćnik koji vraća goli `int` s točkom.
- [LEARN:workflow] Prije rada na postu koji već ima izvještaj u `quality_reports/<slug>_qa.md`, pročitati taj izvještaj. Popravci opisani u njemu znali su nedostajati u radnoj datoteci, pa su isti nalazi pronađeni dvaput (*gotovo pet puta*, *Rastu i kad naplata stoji*, dugačka crtica u [KUT]).
